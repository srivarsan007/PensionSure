## Background: The 4th DLC Campaign (November 2025)

In November 2025, the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances & Pensions launched its **4th nationwide Digital Life Certificate (DLC) Campaign** to assist pensioners in submitting annual life certificates through:
- The Jeevan Pramaan portal
- Face authentication technology (AadhaarFaceRD app)
- Biometric scanners at bank branches and post offices

---

## Biometric & Technical Challenges Documented

### A. Authentication Failures

| Challenge | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Fingerprint degradation** | Age-related changes in fingerprint texture cause scanner mismatches in elderly pensioners |
| **Facial recognition failure** | Poor lighting, movement during scan, background clutter cause face auth failures |
| **Iris scanner compatibility** | Not all registered RD devices work uniformly across platforms |

### B. Data Mismatch Issues

- Name / date of birth / PPO number mismatch between Aadhaar records and PDA (bank/post office) records
- Rejected certificates required pensioners to physically correct discrepancies at PDAs before regenerating DLC

### C. Technical / Platform Issues

- Device compatibility failures with older Android versions
- AadhaarFaceRD app stability issues on low-RAM devices
- Real-time Aadhaar authentication requiring stable internet connectivity (problematic in rural areas)

### D. Specific Group Challenges

**NRI Pensioners:**
- No access to Indian mobile number for OTP verification
- Unable to complete Aadhaar biometric authentication from abroad

**Elderly / Technologically Challenged:**
- High dependency on family members or external agents
- Exploitation risk when using unauthorized intermediaries

---

## Government Response Measures

| Measure | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Mega-camps | 2,000+ districts and cities; 19 banks, 1,600 post offices participated |
| Doorstep service | IPPB and department teams visited aged/sick/incapacitated pensioners |
| Correction guidance | Pensioners directed to PDAs to correct data mismatches before regeneration |
| Grievance redressal | CPENGRAMS portal for formal complaints |
| Security advisories | Warning against sharing Aadhaar details with unauthorized agents |
