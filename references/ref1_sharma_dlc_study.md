## Key Themes Covered in DLC Research Literature

### 1. Problem Context
The Digital Life Certificate (DLC) was introduced to allow pensioners to submit their annual life certificate digitally via the Jeevan Pramaan portal, eliminating the need to physically visit pension disbursing agencies (banks, post offices).

### 2. Challenges Identified
- **Biometric Failure:** Age-related fingerprint degradation and facial feature changes cause authentication failures in elderly pensioners
- **Digital Divide:** Large segments of the pensioner population lack digital literacy or device access
- **Connectivity:** Rural and NRI pensioners face internet connectivity constraints for real-time Aadhaar authentication
- **Data Mismatch:** Name, date of birth, and PPO number mismatches between entered data and PDA records cause rejections

### 3. Proposed Solutions in Literature
- AI/ML-based alternative authentication (video life certification)
- Blockchain-enabled tamper-proof pension certificate management
- Mobile-based biometric scanning without external devices
