## What Is Jeevan Pramaan?

**Jeevan Pramaan** is the official Government of India portal for the **Digital Life Certificate (DLC)** service. It is a biometric-enabled digital service designed to simplify the life certificate submission process for pensioners.

### Purpose
- Eliminate the need for pensioners to physically visit pension disbursing agencies (banks, post offices)
- Enable Aadhaar-based biometric/face authentication from home
- Cover pensioners from Central Government, State Governments, Defence, PSUs, and other government organizations

---

## Key Features

### Authentication Methods
1. **Face Authentication** — via Android smartphone using the AadhaarFaceRD app; no external device needed
2. **Biometric Devices** — fingerprint or iris scanners with registered RD (Registered Device) service

### Process Flow
1. Pensioner opens the Jeevan Pramaan app / portal
2. Enters Aadhaar number, PPO number, and bank account details
3. Completes biometric/face authentication
4. A unique **Pramaan ID** is generated
5. The DLC is stored on the portal and electronically accessed by the Pension Disbursing Agency (PDA)
6. Pension continues without physical visit

### Accessibility
- Available on Android smartphones (Google Play Store)
- Mega-camps organized across 2,000+ districts for assisted submission
- Doorstep services via India Post Payments Bank (IPPB) for elderly/disabled
