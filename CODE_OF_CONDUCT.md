# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone who contributes to or uses PensionSure, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards of Behavior

Participants are expected to:
- Exercise empathy and kindness in all interactions.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive technical feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Respect differing perspectives and experiences.

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or exclusionary comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional context.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior may be reported confidentially to the maintainers via the repository's [Private Security Advisory portal](https://github.com/srivarsan007/PensionSure/security/advisories).

All complaints will be reviewed promptly and impartially. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
